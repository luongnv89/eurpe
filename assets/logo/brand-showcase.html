EURPE · Brand Identity

EU Research Project Proposal Expert — a fully-local, privacy-respecting AI assistant for drafting Horizon Europe and related research proposals.

Design concept

The mark stacks three horizontal bars to suggest both the letter E and the layered structure of a proposal — sections, deliverables, work packages, evidence. The single gold dot at the mid-bar's edge is the EU-funding accent: a visual nod to the ring of stars, and a "highlight" that signals the indexed cursor / the spot where the proposal coordinator is working. Geometry is deliberately reduced so the mark stays legible from 16×16 favicon to hero banner.

Usage rules

  • Gold is an accent only — never a fill or background. One dot per mark.
  • Never recolor the bars to gold or invert the navy/gold relationship.
  • Maintain at least 8px of clear space around the mark at any size.
  • Use logo-white.svg on backgrounds darker than #444; logo-black.svg for print or monochrome contexts.
  • At 16×16 use favicon.svg (2-layer simplification) — the full mark is unreadable.
